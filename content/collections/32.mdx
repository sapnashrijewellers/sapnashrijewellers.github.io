# What Are Oxidised Silver Rings?

Oxidised silver rings are silver jewellery pieces featuring a deliberately darkened finish that creates an antique or vintage-inspired appearance.

In oxidised jewellery, darker detailing generally settles into recessed areas of the design, while raised portions retain a brighter silver appearance. This contrast highlights intricate patterns, engraved details and traditional motifs.

The oxidised finish gives silver rings a distinctive look that is especially popular in traditional Indian jewellery, tribal-inspired designs and men's statement accessories.

Unlike plain polished silver rings, oxidised silver rings have a more rustic and antique appearance. They are a great choice for men who prefer bold jewellery with traditional character.

## 1. What is an oxidised silver ring?

## 2. Are oxidised silver rings made from real silver?

## 3. What is a 925 oxidised silver ring?

A 925 oxidised silver ring is made using sterling silver containing 92.5% pure silver, with the remaining 7.5% generally consisting of other metals. It features an oxidised finish that gives the ring its distinctive antique appearance.

## 4. Are oxidised silver rings suitable for men?

Yes, oxidised silver rings are popular men's jewellery choices. Their bold patterns, antique finishes and traditional designs make them suitable for everyday styling and special occasions.

## 5. What is the difference between oxidised silver and normal silver rings?

The main difference is the finish. Normal silver rings generally have a bright polished appearance, while oxidised silver rings feature darker detailing that creates an antique-style look.

## 6. Do oxidised silver rings lose their black finish?

The darkened appearance can gradually change with regular wear, friction and cleaning. Raised areas may become brighter over time, while recessed areas can retain darker detailing. Proper jewellery care helps maintain the appearance.

## 7. How do I clean and maintain an oxidised silver ring?

Clean your oxidised ring gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive polishing and aggressive silver-cleaning solutions, as they can remove the darkened detailing. Follow the care instructions provided for your particular ring.

## 8. Are oxidised silver rings suitable for daily wear?

Yes, many oxidised silver rings can be worn daily depending on their design, weight and comfort. Choose a well-fitted ring and take care to avoid excessive contact with chemicals and abrasive surfaces.

## 9. What is Chandi Ki Anguthi in oxidised jewellery?

Chandi Ki Anguthi is the Hindi term for a silver ring. An oxidised Chandi Ki Anguthi features a darkened antique-style finish and is a popular choice for men who appreciate traditional silver jewellery.
