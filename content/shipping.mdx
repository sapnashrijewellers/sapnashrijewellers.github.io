## Undelivered Shipments & Re-dispatch

Customers are responsible for providing complete and accurate shipping details and ensuring that someone is available to receive the shipment at the provided address.

If a shipment is returned to Sapna Shri Jewellers by the courier partner due to any of the following reasons:

- Incorrect, incomplete, or invalid delivery address.
- Address not found or location not traceable.
- Customer or recipient unavailable at the time of delivery.
- Customer or recipient refusing to accept the shipment.
- Failure to respond to courier delivery calls or coordinate with the delivery partner.
- Any other reason attributable to the customer or recipient.

The following conditions shall apply:

- If the customer requests re-dispatch of the returned shipment, **the additional shipping and courier charges for sending the order again must be borne by the customer**.
- Re-dispatch will be arranged only after the customer confirms the corrected delivery details and pays the applicable additional shipping charges.
- The customer must ensure that the revised address and contact details are accurate and that someone is available to receive the shipment.
- Re-dispatch timelines will depend on payment of additional shipping charges and courier service availability.
- Sapna Shri Jewellers reserves the right to verify the returned shipment before arranging re-dispatch.

**Important:** Any additional shipping charges arising from a returned shipment due to reasons attributable to the customer are separate from the original shipping charges and will not be absorbed by Sapna Shri Jewellers.

Where a shipment is returned due to a courier partner's error or an issue attributable to Sapna Shri Jewellers, the matter will be reviewed separately, and the customer will not automatically be charged for re-dispatch.

## Cancellation Policy

Sapna Shri Jewellers does **not encourage cancellations** once an order is placed, as jewellery orders involve preparation, reservation, or allocation of inventory.

Cancellation requests may be considered **only under the following limited conditions**:

- Duplicate orders placed by the same customer for the same product and quantity within **24 hours**.
- Cancellation request raised **within 12 hours** of order placement and **before shipment creation**.

❗ **No cancellation requests will be accepted once the order has been shipped or prepared for dispatch.**

Refunds, if applicable, are processed only after internal verification and may take reasonable processing time.

- Accompanied by the original invoice and packaging.
